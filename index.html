A SMALL GUIDE

Make room for quiet.

This is an artistic interpretation of Ryōan-ji’s dry landscape garden, seen from beneath the timber eaves. There is no score, and no correct arrangement.

Shape the gravel

Drag with Rake, Raise, Press, Push, or Smooth. The rake bar turns with your drag and leaves parallel grooves. Push moves material in the direction of your stroke. [ and ] change the rake width or brush diameter.

Compose with stone

Choose Stones, then click the gravel. Drag existing stones to reposition them. R rotates the selected stone; Delete removes it.

Change your view

Right-drag to look around; scroll to zoom. Switch Perspective for a higher view. Contemplate hides the tools; Esc brings them back.

Keep a moment

Save keeps this garden in this browser and restores it on your next visit. Ctrl / ⌘ + Z undoes a stroke or a stone edit.

Looking to Kyoto

The low clay walls, white gravel, moss islands, fifteen stones in five groups, and shaded veranda draw from these landscape and stone references. The playable layout and sculptural forms are freely interpreted.
